• A fresh, easier to read look: one blue across the app, smaller headings and most texts no longer in capitals.
• Home shows the next training with its length, your coach and the time left until it, and your goal and plan in whole sentences.
• Chat now opens from a button in the top bar, so it no longer covers anything.
• Coaches enter sets faster: bigger fields, last time's best set next to each exercise and +1 rep, +2.5 kg and Repeat set buttons.
• Training progress starts with the last 30 days compared to the 30 before, and every chart shows how much it changed.
• Workouts show the muscles they work, workouts without a picture get an icon of their category, and coaches find import in the + menu.
• A new training starts as soon as the coach writes its name, and the form shows the time running, so the start is right even when the form stays open.
